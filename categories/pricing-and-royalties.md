# Game Engine Pricing & Royalties Comparison

Understanding total cost of ownership, seat licenses, revenue thresholds, royalties, and console fees, prevents painful surprises after launch.

- **Last Verified:** 2026-11-16

## Pricing & Royalty Table

| Engine | Upfront / Free Tier | Commercial Threshold or Paid Tier | Royalty | Official Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **[Godot](../engines/godot.md)** | $0 | Always $0 | **0%** | [godotengine.org/license](https://godotengine.org/license/) |
| **[Defold](../engines/defold.md)** | $0 | Always $0 (charter-guaranteed) | **0%** | [defold.com/license](https://defold.com/license/) |
| **[GDevelop](../engines/gdevelop.md)** | $0 | Always $0 for local exports (cloud plans ~$4.99–$9.99/mo) | **0%** | [gdevelop.io/pricing](https://gdevelop.io/pricing) |
| **[Phaser](../engines/phaser.md)** | $0 | Always $0 (MIT) | **0%** | [phaser.io](https://phaser.io/) |
| **[Bevy](../engines/bevy.md)** | $0 | Always $0 (MIT/Apache 2.0) | **0%** | [bevyengine.org](https://bevyengine.org/) |
| **[Ren'Py](../engines/renpy.md)** | $0 | Always $0 (MIT) | **0%** | [renpy.org](https://www.renpy.org/doc/html/license.html) |
| **Cocos Creator** | $0 | Always $0 (optional enterprise support) | **0%** | [cocos.com](https://www.cocos.com/en/creator) |
| **[Unity](../engines/unity.md)** | $0 (Personal) | Pro ($2,310/seat/yr) above **$200k/yr** revenue/funding or consoles | **0%** (Runtime Fee canceled) | [unity.com/pricing](https://unity.com/pricing) |
| **[Unreal Engine](../engines/unreal-engine.md)** | $0 | Royalty triggers after **$1M lifetime gross revenue** per game | **5%** (> $1M; 0% on EGS) | [unrealengine.com/eula](https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US/eula/unreal) |
| **[GameMaker](../engines/gamemaker.md)** | $0 (non-commercial) | **$99.99 one-time** (PC/Mobile/Web); **$79.99/mo** (Consoles) | **0%** | [gamemaker.io/get](https://gamemaker.io/en/get) |
| **[Construct 3](../engines/construct-3.md)** | $0 (25 events) | **~$15.99/mo** or **~$119/yr** | **0%** | [construct.net](https://www.construct.net/en/make-games/buy-construct-3/personal-plans) |
| **RPG Maker MZ** | Paid | **$69.99–$79.99** one-time | **0%** | [rpgmakerofficial.com](https://rpgmakerofficial.com/product/mz/) |
| **CRYENGINE** | $0 | Royalty triggers above **$5,000/yr** revenue | **5%** | [cryengine.com](https://www.cryengine.com/get-cryengine) |
