# Mobile Game Engines (iOS & Android)

Mobile games require small binary sizes, battery-friendly rendering, touch input support, and reliable integration with iOS/Android SDKs (in-app purchases, ads, push notifications).

- **Last Verified:** 2026-11-16

## Best Mobile Game Engines

| Engine | iOS & Android | Binary Footprint | SDK Ecosystem | Pricing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **[Unity](../engines/unity.md)** | Yes | Medium | Largest (ads, IAP, analytics) | Free <$200k/yr; Pro $2,310/seat/yr |
| **[Defold](../engines/defold.md)** | Yes | Very Small | Strong mobile SDK extensions | Free forever (0% royalty) |
| **Cocos Creator** | Yes | Small | Strong (especially casual/APAC) | Free (0% royalty) |
| **[Godot](../engines/godot.md)** | Yes | Small–Medium | Growing (community plugins) | Free (MIT) |
| **Solar2D** | Yes | Very Small | Good for 2D mobile | Free (MIT) |
| **[GDevelop](../engines/gdevelop.md)** | Yes | Medium (Web wrapper) | Built-in mobile basics | Free (paid cloud builds optional) |
| **[Unreal Engine](../engines/unreal-engine.md)** | Yes | Large | Good for high-end 3D mobile | 5% royalty >$1M lifetime |
