# Free Game Engines (Zero Upfront Cost & Zero-Royalty Options)

"Free" can mean two different things: **free to start** (with revenue thresholds or royalties later) or **100% free forever with zero royalties**.

- **Last Verified:** 2026-11-16

## 100% Free Forever & Zero Royalties (Commercial Use Included)

| Engine | License | 2D / 3D | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **[Godot](../engines/godot.md)** | MIT | 2D & 3D | Completely free; third-party middleware for consoles |
| **[GDevelop](../engines/gdevelop.md)** | MIT (core) | 2D & light 3D | Free local exports; optional paid cloud builds |
| **[Defold](../engines/defold.md)** | Defold License | 2D & 3D | Charter-guaranteed free forever, including consoles |
| **[Phaser](../engines/phaser.md)** | MIT | 2D (Web) | Free framework; optional paid Phaser Editor |
| **[Bevy](../engines/bevy.md)** | MIT / Apache 2.0 | 2D & 3D | Free Rust ECS engine |
| **[Ren'Py](../engines/renpy.md)** | MIT | 2D (Visual Novel) | Free for commercial visual novels |
| **Cocos Creator** | Open-source core | 2D & 3D | Free; optional paid enterprise support |
| **LÖVE / MonoGame / libGDX / Stride / O3DE** | zlib / Ms-PL / Apache 2.0 / MIT | 2D / 3D | All 100% free with zero royalties |

## Free to Start (With Revenue Thresholds or Royalties)

- **[Unity](../engines/unity.md):** Free Personal tier up to **$200,000** trailing 12-month revenue/funding.
- **[Unreal Engine](../engines/unreal-engine.md):** Free until your game surpasses **$1,000,000** lifetime gross revenue (then 5% royalty; 0% on Epic Games Store).
- **[GameMaker](../engines/gamemaker.md):** Free for non-commercial use; **$99.99 one-time** to publish commercially on PC/mobile/web.
