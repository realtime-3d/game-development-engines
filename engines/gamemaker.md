# GameMaker

**GameMaker** is a veteran 2D-focused game engine known for rapid prototyping, a clean room/object workflow, and a dual programming model supporting both **GML** (GameMaker Language) and **GML Visual**.

- **Latest Version Checked:** GameMaker (Monthly & LTS)
- **License:** Proprietary ([GameMaker Pricing & Terms](https://gamemaker.io/en/get))
- **Open Source:** No
- **Last Verified:** 2026-11-16

## Best For

- 2D action games, top-down shooters, Metroidvanias, and platformers
- Pixel-art games
- Beginners transitioning from visual blocks to text-based scripting

## Languages & Visual Scripting

- **GML (GameMaker Language):** Fast, C/JS-like scripting language tailored for 2D game logic.
- **GML Visual:** Built-in node/block visual scripting (included free in all tiers) that maps directly to GML functions.

## 2D & 3D Capabilities

- **2D:** Dedicated 2D sprite editor, tilemaps, rooms, sequences, shaders, and fast 2D rendering.
- **3D:** Not a full 3D engine. Supports low-level 3D primitives, vertex buffers, and matrix math for stylized/pseudo-3D effects, but lacks a built-in 3D scene editor or PBR pipeline.

## Editor & Target Platforms

- **Editor runs on:** Windows, macOS
- **Export targets:**
  - Free & Professional: Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS, Web (HTML5), GX.games
  - Enterprise: PlayStation 4/5, Xbox Series X/S/One, Nintendo Switch (with platform-holder approval)

## Pricing & License Terms

Per [GameMaker's official pricing](https://gamemaker.io/en/get):

| Tier | Price | Commercial Use | Platforms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | **$0** | Non-commercial only | Desktop, Mobile, Web, GX.games |
| **Professional** | **$99.99 one-time** | Commercial (0% royalties) | Desktop, Mobile, Web, GX.games |
| **Enterprise** | **$79.99/mo** (or $799.99/yr) | Commercial (0% royalties) | Adds PlayStation, Xbox, Switch |

## Pros & Limitations

**Pros:**
- Very fast 2D iteration loop from idea to playable prototype.
- One-time $99.99 commercial license for PC/mobile/web with zero royalties.
- Long track record of shipped commercial indie games.

**Limitations:**
- Free tier cannot be used for commercial releases.
- Not designed for 3D games.
- Console exports require the $79.99/mo Enterprise subscription.
